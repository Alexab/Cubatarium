# Privacy Policy — Cubatarium

**Last updated:** 2026-06-22

Cubatarium is an offline sandbox game. This policy describes how the application handles information.

## Data collection

Cubatarium **does not collect, transmit, or sell** personal data. The app:

- works **offline** (no `INTERNET` permission on Android);
- does not use analytics, advertising, or crash reporting SDKs;
- does not require user accounts.

## Data stored on your device

Game worlds, settings, and progress are saved **locally** on your device. This data is not sent to the developer or third parties.

## Third-party content

The game includes open-source libraries and community resource packs with different licenses.

- Cubatarium source code: MIT.
- Bundled assets/libraries: may include MIT, Apache-2.0, CC0, CC BY, and CC BY-SA components.
- Full attribution and license mapping: [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Children

Cubatarium does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The current version is maintained in the project repository.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the project repository or contact the developer email listed in the store listing.
